## 🧠 What is NEXUSFLOW AI?

Supply-chain disruptions rarely arrive as structured database
events.

They arrive as:

- 📧 Supplier emails
- 🚚 Carrier delay notifications
- 🏭 Production-halt notices
- 🏢 Warehouse incident reports
- 💬 Unstructured operational messages

The real challenge is not simply detecting a disruption.

The challenge is determining:

> **What does this disruption actually affect?**

NEXUSFLOW AI converts an unstructured disruption notice into a
complete, evidence-backed operational assessment.

```text
UNSTRUCTURED NOTICE
        ↓
    UNDERSTAND
        ↓
      RESOLVE
        ↓
       TRACE
        ↓
     QUANTIFY
        ↓
     SIMULATE
        ↓
    RECOMMEND
        ↓
     EXPLAIN
        ↓
    ESCALATE
```
